Benchmark reviews

with Epoch AI

  1. Flawed 29 Sep 2026

    OSWorld 2.1

    I think these kinds of tasks are clearly saturated. Models seem to struggle with longer tasks, but I believe that's context rot or an issue with long-context reasoning, not a lack of intelligence or bad computer use. Benchmarking is hard, and this was a good attempt for early-2026 models. We need to move on to harder stuff.

  2. Verified 12 Sep 2026

    ExploitBench↗

    Very narrow, but decently predictive of exploit-development ability. Let's keep an eye on the open-source models, especially since their low cost makes it possible to scale the number of tokens spent per task massively.

  3. Flawed 10 Sep 2026

    HealthBench Professional↗

    I think models are better at answering healthcare questions than evaluations show. They clearly still have nuanced, niche failures, so a professional should always check their answers, but the floor is incredibly high. I'm super excited about billions of people having this technology in their hands.

  4. Verified 9 Sep 2026

    PostTrainBench↗

    Cool concept, and well implemented. Good job on the updates and on addressing issues promptly. I'd love a new version that gives models more freedom, with tasks closer to what humans go through and similar budgets.
